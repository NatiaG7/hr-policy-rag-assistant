EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK - SAMPLE POLICY DOCUMENT

SECTION 1: EMPLOYEE BENEFITS

All full-time employees are eligible for health insurance, dental coverage, and vision plans
after 30 days of employment. The company contributes 80% of premium costs for employee coverage.

Paid time off includes 15 vacation days per year, 10 sick days, and recognized public holidays.
Employees may also participate in a 401(k) retirement plan with company matching up to 4% of salary.

SECTION 2: WORKING CONDITIONS

The company is committed to a safe and healthy workplace. Flexible working arrangements,
including remote work up to two days per week, may be approved by department managers.

Professional development opportunities include annual training budgets and tuition reimbursement
for job-related courses up to $2,500 per year.

SECTION 3: CODE OF CONDUCT

Employees must follow all company policies regarding confidentiality, anti-harassment,
and equal opportunity employment. Violations may result in disciplinary action up to termination.

SECTION 4: LEAVE POLICY

Parental leave of up to 12 weeks is available for eligible employees. Bereavement leave
of up to 5 days is granted for immediate family members.

For questions about benefits or HR policies, contact the Human Resources department at hr@company.example.
